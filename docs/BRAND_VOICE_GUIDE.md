# Dao DeGen Brand & Voice Guide

**Last updated:** 2026-02-15  
**Owner:** Surf (brand + media)  
**Distribution:** @srsmoneybizness (brand) · @Potatoofdoom (founder voice)

---

## 1. Identity Snapshot
- **Mantra:** "x·y = k doesn't care about your feelings." The liquidity curve is impartial; we observe, report, and laugh from the calm center.
- **Archetype:** Deadpan oracle + reformed shitposter. We are the person who blew up on 100× leverage, read Lao Tzu, and came back wiser but still sarcastic.
- **Promise:** 81 verses of crypto Zen + daily accountability. No hype, no cope, just disciplined posting with a philosophical wink.
- **Visual cues:** Dark gradients, amber highlights, verse cards with ample breathing room, tiny x·y=k watermark.

## 2. Tone & Personality
| Mode | When to use | Voice cues |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Stoic math teacher** | Explaining mechanics, safety, revenue splits | Precise wording, references to invariants/constants, short sentences |
| **Deadpan shitposter** | CT replies, launch-day zingers | Dry humor, sarcasm, confident one-liners |
| **Sincere sage** | Verse drops, long-form reflections | Calm pacing, gentle encouragement, no slang |
| **Gremlin hype** | VTuber clips, live show call-to-action | Playful chaos, self-aware memes, limit to <10% of posts |

**Voice checkpoints before publishing:**
1. Would this line make sense if read over a lo-fi beat? (rhythm)  
2. Does it remind people that markets do not care? (perspective)  
3. Is there at least one concrete detail (verse number, hook name, wallet action)? (credibility)

## 3. Do / Don't Rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Tie every post back to a verse, invariant, or tactical next step. | Drift into generic "gm" or wagmi platitudes. |
| Make jokes that reward people who understand AMMs, onchain culture, or Tao references. | Punch down at users, projects, or specific losses. No schadenfreude. |
| Use numbers, schedules, and receipts ("Verse 13 drops 14:00 UTC"). | Promise price action, "moon" outcomes, or implied returns. |
| Admit uncertainty, invite observation: "Watcher of liquidity." | Command people to buy/mint; no imperatives like "ape now." |
| Credit collaborators (Rock QA, VTuber Sprotogremlin, etc.). | Leak internal/pending deals or unreleased tech. |

## 4. Taboo Claims & Compliance Guardrails
- **No financial advice.** Spell it out once per thread if value adjacent: "Not an offer; read, don't ape."
- **No guarantees on NFTs, hook yield, DAO membership, or revenue sharing.** Use "target", "goal", "experiment" — never "will" about financial outcomes.
- **No unregistered securities language.** Avoid "invest", "returns", "dividends" unless quoting regulations and caveating.
- **No chain agnostic slander.** We can poke fun at ourselves, not competitors' failures.
- **No personal data.** Do not reference wallet addresses, mints, or user behavior without consent.
- **When in doubt:** default to describing what *we* are doing (shipping a verse, publishing docs) instead of what *you* should do.

Add this footer (or variant) on every promotional or CTA-style post:
> "Dao DeGen is an art + research project. Nothing here is financial advice."

## 5. Posting Checklist
1. **Hook:** Lead with a verse fragment, data point, or problem statement.
2. **Anchor:** Name the verse number / NFT slot / hook behavior.
3. **Insight:** One sentence connecting Tao wisdom to a real crypto pain.
4. **Action:** Soft CTA ("Read Verse 12", "Tune into the Space") + link.
5. **Guardrail:** Disclaimer snippet if anything could be misread as an offering.
