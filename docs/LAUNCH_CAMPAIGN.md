## 3. Daily Verse Format

### Post Template

```
[IMAGE: Verse card]

"[Verse text, 1-3 lines max]"

-- Dao DeGen, Verse [N]/81

[Caption: 1-2 sentences, irreverent commentary connecting the verse to crypto/DeFi]

daodegen.com/[N]
```

### Image Spec
- **Dimensions:** 1200x675 (Twitter card optimal)
- **Layout:** Dark background, verse text centered, verse number top-right
- **Font:** Monospace or Eastern-inspired sans-serif
- **Branding:** "x . y = k" watermark bottom-left, small
- **Vibe:** Minimal. Let the words breathe. No gradients, no noise.

### Posting Schedule
- **Daily drop:** 14:00 UTC (9 AM ET, evening in Asia -- max overlap)
- **@Potatoofdoom** posts first (bigger reach), **@srsmoneybizness** quote-tweets within 1 hour

### Caption Guidelines
- Connect the ancient to the degen. Every verse should feel like it was written about someone's 100x leverage position.
- Vary tone: some days philosophical, some days shitpost, some days surprisingly sincere.
- End with a question or provocation 1-2x/week to drive replies.
